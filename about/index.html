Our Mission

Getting someone to call or visit your website is only the beginning. What happens next matters.

Your next customer could reach out while you’re on a job, helping someone else or finished for the day. When a call goes unanswered, many people simply try the next business.

Vocal Crest sets up AI assistants that welcome callers, respond to website visitors, collect enquiry details and help customers book appointments. We tailor each assistant to your services, your availability and the way you work.

Our Service Philosophy

You get a managed service with people behind it

Vocal Crest is not a generic plug-in left for you to figure out. We handle the setup and ongoing management so you stay focused on your customers.

01 / APPROVED INFORMATION

Built on details you approve

We set up each assistant around the details you approve—your services, your opening hours, and your common customer questions—so callers and visitors get accurate answers.

02 / HUMAN HANDOVER

Keep people involved where it matters

You set clear rules for when an enquiry should reach you or your staff. When a person needs to step in, the enquiry is routed to your team with the customer’s details.

03 / ONGOING MANAGEMENT

Tested & updated with you

We configure your AI receptionist, website chatbot or website around your business, test everything with you before launch, and update it as your business needs change.
