About Us

Helping businesses turn more conversations into customers.

Vocal Crest helps businesses across Canada and the United States answer calls, engage website visitors and book appointments through AI receptionists and chat solutions.

Behind the business are two brothers, Ali and Omar, with a shared interest in helping business owners grow without adding more to their day.

Our story

We created Vocal Crest around a simple idea: being busy running your business shouldn’t stop you from connecting with your next customer.

When you’re serving a customer, out on a job or finished for the evening, new enquiries can still come in. We saw an opportunity to use AI to help businesses respond during those moments.

Today, we help businesses put AI to practical use, from answering calls while their teams are busy to assisting website visitors after hours. We focus on making those first interactions helpful, relevant and easy for customers.

Our Approach

Built around the way you work

Every business has its own services, customers and approach to communication. We take the time to understand yours.

We configure your assistant using information you approve, establish how bookings and enquiries should be handled, and create a clear path to your team when a customer needs personal attention.

As your business changes, we help keep your setup current.
